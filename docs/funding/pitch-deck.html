The Problem

Nigeria's fiscal data is buried and broken

Government spending data across 36 states exists only in scattered PDFs, scanned documents, and opaque portals. No one can access, query, or analyze it at scale.

⚠ Inaccessible Data

700+ budget documents spread across 37 states in PDF/XLSX format. No unified database. No API. No way to search or compare across states.

⚠ No Credit Intelligence

Banks lend billions to state governments without standardized fiscal health data. Credit agencies lack structured government financial profiles.

⚠ Opaque Spending

891,000+ federal contractor payments with no searchable interface. Citizens, journalists, and researchers can't track where money goes.

⚠ Research Bottleneck

NGOs, consultants, and media spend weeks manually extracting data from government PDFs. Every fiscal analysis starts from scratch.
