## About Ajim Capital

Ajim Capital is an early-stage venture fund investing in technology companies building Africa's core infrastructure. They back pre-seed and seed-stage startups across Sub-Saharan Africa.

### Fund History
- **Fund I:** $10M — invested in 20+ startups, check sizes up to $250K
- **Fund II:** Currently active — check sizes of **$250K–$500K** at pre-seed and seed (total fund size undisclosed)
- Had their **first exit in 2024**

### Investment Thesis
Built on Africa's demographic advantage:
- 75% of Africa's 1.4B people are under 35
- 500M+ internet users, 93% mobile penetration
- 3-6% annual GDP growth projected across most African economies

### The "5 T's" — Investment Criteria
1. **Team** — Solid founders with background, experience, and skills to execute

3. **Timing** — Large market with right timing
4. **Technology** — Good offering solving an urgent, pervasive, valuable market problem
5. **Traction** — Product-market fit indicators: **$5K–$100K MRR, growing 10% MoM**

## Pitch Strategy

### One-Liner
**"AI-powered government data infrastructure for Africa — turning 700+ budget documents into enterprise intelligence for media, financial institutions, and research firms."**

### Positioning
- Lead with **OurNigeria Research** (the for-profit). The Foundation is the social impact story, not the investment thesis.
- Verticals to check on application: **AI & Automation** and **B2B SaaS**
- Frame as **data infrastructure**, not civic tech
- The civic platform is a distribution/brand engine that feeds the enterprise funnel (like how Duolingo's free tier feeds premium)

### Key Pitch Points
1. **Problem:** Nigerian government fiscal data is trapped in 700+ PDFs across 37 states. No structured, queryable source exists. Banks lending to governments, credit agencies, media houses, and researchers all need this data — and nobody provides it.
2. **Solution:** AI-powered pipeline that ingests, structures, and serves government fiscal data via API. Multi-agent LLM system enables natural language queries across all 37 states.
3. **Moat:** 708K+ vector embeddings, 959 source documents, 37 states covered. First mover. Extremely hard to replicate.
4. **Business model:** B2B data API subscriptions, data licensing, commissioned research, credit intelligence products, media partnerships with revenue share.
5. **Traction:** Platform live. Full AI pipeline operational. 37/37 states ingested. Chat interface working. Telegram bot live. (Pre-revenue — investment accelerates go-to-market.)
6. **Ask:** $250K–$500K seed to hire first 2 team members (backend dev + content writer), build enterprise API layer, and close first 3-5 paying customers.
7. **Use of funds:** 40% engineering (enterprise API, premium features), 30% go-to-market (first 5 customers), 20% content & distribution, 10% ops.

### Traction Metrics to Highlight (Non-Revenue)
- 37 states covered (100% of Nigeria)
- 700+ budget documents ingested
- 708K+ vector embeddings
- 959 source files processed
- Multi-agent AI system with 5+ specialist agents
- Working chat interface (English + Pidgin)
- Telegram bot integration
- 22 chart types for data visualization

### Customer Pipeline to Present
- **Tier 1 (quick wins):** TechPoint, TechCabal, IntelPoint — media houses with tech infra to integrate APIs
- **Tier 2 (high value):** CRC, CreditRegistry, FirstCentral — credit rating agencies that need government fiscal data
- **Tier 3 (longer cycle):** Banks (Premium Trust, Sterling), MFBs, insurance companies
