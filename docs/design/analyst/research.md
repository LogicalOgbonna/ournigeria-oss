## 1. Internally Generated Revenue (IGR)

**Publisher:** National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)
**Update frequency:** Annual
**Format:** PDF (state-by-state breakdowns with tax revenue + MDA revenue components)
**Coverage:** All 37 states (36 + FCT), with breakdowns by PAYE, direct assessment, road taxes, stamp duties, capital gains tax, withholding taxes, other taxes, and LGA revenue.

### Download Links

| Year | Link |
|------|------|
| **2024** | [NBS Microdata Portal — 2024 IGR Report](https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/170) |
| 2023 | [NBS — IGR 2023 (PDF)](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/pdfuploads/IGR_2023.pdf) |
| 2022 | [NBS E-Library — IGR 2022](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/1241400) |
| 2019-2021 | [NBS E-Library — IGR 2019-2021](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/1241239) |
| All years | [NBS E-Library search: IGR](https://nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary?queries%5Bsearch%5D=IGR) |

### Key Stats (2024)
- Total: N3.63 trillion (49.7% YoY growth from N2.43T in 2023)
- Top 5: Lagos (N1.26T), Rivers (N317.3B), FCT (N282.4B), Ogun (N194.9B), Enugu (N180.5B)
- PAYE was largest tax component: N1.86T (69.84% of total taxes)

### Notes
- Nigeria's last actual census was in 2006. All current state-level figures are NPC/NBS projections based on assumed growth rates.
- A new census has been repeatedly postponed. Population data should always be flagged as "projection" in the system.
- World Bank estimates can serve as a cross-validation source.

## 4. GDP by State (Subnational GDP)

**Publisher:** NBS
**Update frequency:** Irregular (no updates since May 2019)
**Format:** PDF
**Coverage:** Only 22 of 37 states (Phase I: 11 states, Phase II: 11 more)

| Resource | Link |
|----------|------|
| **NBS — States Nominal GDP 2013-2017 (PDF)** | [NBS — State GDP Report (May 2019)](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/pdfuploads/State_Nominal_GDP_2013_-_2017.cdr_(MAY_2019).pdf) |
| NBS E-Library entry | [NBS E-Library — States Nominal GDP 2013-2017](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/947) |
| Nigeria Open Data Portal | [Open Data — States Nominal GDP 2013-2017](https://nigeria.opendataforafrica.org/qxjbkcf/states-nominal-gross-domestic-product-2013-2017) |
| National GDP (quarterly, not by state) | [NBS Microdata — GDP Catalog](https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/147) |

### WARNING: This is the problem dataset

- **Last updated:** May 2019 (covering 2013-2017)
- **Only 22 of 37 states** covered across two phases
- NBS has not released updated state-level GDP data due to resource constraints and the 2025 national GDP rebasing exercise
- Phase I states (11): Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Kaduna, Kano, Ogun, Osun, Oyo, Rivers, Zamfara
- Phase II states (11): Not fully documented in public sources

### Recommended Workarounds

1. **Proxy allocation:** Use national GDP + state IGR contribution ratios to estimate state GDP shares
2. **Use stale data with flag:** Ingest the 2013-2017 data but mark all records with `isEstimate: true` and `source: "NBS_2019_report"`
3. **Defer:** Drop debt-to-GDP ratio from Phase 0 scope; add it when NBS eventually publishes updated state GDP
4. **Zone-level aggregation:** NBS national GDP reports include sector breakdowns — could map geopolitical zones to approximate state GDP
