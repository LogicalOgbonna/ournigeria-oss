**Entity:** OurNigeria Research Ltd (For-Profit)
**Priority:** Phase 2
**Timeline:** Month 6-9
**Revenue potential:** $5,000-20,000 per commissioned report; $2,000/month subscription

---

## Problem

International and large domestic companies making investment decisions in Nigeria need to understand the political economy of specific states:

- **Site selection:** Should we build a factory in Kano or Ogun? Which state has better infrastructure spending, fiscal stability, and governance quality?
- **Market entry:** We're launching in 5 Nigerian states. Which have the most government spending on our sector (health, education, agriculture)?
- **Government relations:** We want to partner with a state government on a PPP. Which states have the fiscal capacity and track record to honour commitments?
- **Risk assessment:** Our supply chain runs through 3 northern states. What's the fiscal stability outlook? Are there corruption red flags?

Today, companies hire expensive consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, PwC) who spend weeks manually collecting the same data OurNigeria already has structured.

---

## Solution

**State Investment Readiness Reports** — comprehensive political economy assessments for specific states or comparative analyses across states.

### Report Structure

1. **Fiscal Health Overview** — Revenue, expenditure, debt, FAAC dependency (from A3)
2. **Sector Spending Analysis** — How much does this state spend on the client's sector? Trend?
3. **Infrastructure Spending** — Roads, power, water allocation and execution rates
4. **Payment Discipline** — Does the state pay contractors? How quickly? (from A1)
5. **Governance Quality** — Budget transparency, corruption cases, procurement patterns
6. **Comparative Ranking** — How does this state compare to alternatives on each dimension?
7. **Risk Factors** — Specific risks identified from the data (declining revenue, rising debt, corruption patterns)
8. **Opportunity Map** — Sectors with rising government investment, unmet needs

### Delivery Formats

- **Commissioned reports** — Bespoke 20-40 page reports tailored to client's question ($5K-20K each)
- **Self-service dashboard** — Client accesses state comparison tools directly ($2K/month subscription)
- **Quarterly briefings** — Subscription with quarterly state fiscal health updates ($3K/month)
