---

## Problem

Nigeria faces severe climate challenges:
- **Flooding:** 2022 floods displaced 1.4 million people; 2024 floods equally devastating
- **Desertification:** 11 northern states losing arable land at 0.6km/year
- **Erosion:** Southeastern states (Anambra, Imo, Enugu) suffer devastating gully erosion
- **Deforestation:** Nigeria has one of the highest deforestation rates in the world

Nigeria has committed to:
- Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement
- Climate Change Act 2021 (established National Council on Climate Change)
- Net-zero emissions by 2060
- Energy Transition Plan ($410B over 30 years)

Yet nobody tracks whether government budgets reflect these commitments:
- How much do states allocate to environment, erosion control, flood prevention?
- What's the budget execution rate for environmental programs?
- Which states are investing in climate adaptation vs ignoring it?
- How does Nigeria's climate spending compare to its NDC commitments?

---

## Solution

A **Climate Spending Tracker** — filtered view of the Sector Accountability Dashboard.

### Climate-Relevant Budget Categories

| Category | Budget Sectors | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Environment & Ecology** | Ministry of Environment budget items | Direct environmental protection spending |
| **Erosion & Flood Control** | Infrastructure: erosion control, drainage, flood barriers | Nigeria's #1 climate vulnerability |
| **Reforestation & Land Management** | Agriculture/Environment: forestry programs | Combating desertification and deforestation |
| **Renewable Energy** | Energy sector: solar, wind, mini-grid programs | Energy transition commitment |
| **Water Resources** | Water resources: dam management, irrigation, WASH | Climate adaptation infrastructure |
| **Disaster Management** | NEMA/SEMA allocations: disaster preparedness and response | Climate resilience |

### Dashboard Features

1. **State climate spending rankings** — Which states allocate most/least to climate
2. **Climate vulnerability correlation** — Do high-risk states spend more? (Spoiler: often no)
3. **NDC commitment tracking** — National spending vs Paris Agreement commitments
4. **Execution gap analysis** — Allocated vs spent on environmental programs
5. **Per-capita environmental spending** — Normalised comparison across states
6. **Trend analysis** — Is climate spending keeping pace with climate risk?

## Grant Application Narrative

> "As climate disasters intensify across Nigeria — flooding that displaces millions, erosion that swallows communities, desertification that threatens food security — the question is not whether government should spend on climate adaptation, but whether it actually does. The OurNigeria Climate Spending Tracker is the first tool to answer this question with data, tracking environmental budget allocations and execution across all 37 Nigerian states. It reveals the gap between climate commitments and climate action — and equips advocates with evidence to close it."
