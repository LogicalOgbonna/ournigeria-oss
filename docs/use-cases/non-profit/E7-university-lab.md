# E7: University Open Data Lab

**Entity:** OurNigeria Foundation (Non-Profit)
**Priority:** Phase 2
**Timeline:** Month 6-9
**Impact potential:** Medium (capacity building, long-term civic culture)

---

## Problem

Nigerian university students in political science, economics, public administration, development studies, and journalism write thousands of theses and papers annually on governance topics. The data collection process is brutal:

- Spend 2-4 weeks locating relevant budget documents (often by visiting government offices in person)
- Manually extract figures from 200+ page PDFs
- Build spreadsheets by hand
- Lose weeks to data that turns out to be incomplete or incorrectly transcribed

This isn't a minor inconvenience — it fundamentally limits the quality and scope of Nigerian academic research on governance. A PhD student who could analyze budget trends across all 37 states instead analyzes 3-5 because data collection for more is infeasible.

Meanwhile, OurNigeria has already structured all of this data.

---

## Solution

A **free academic research workspace** providing university students and faculty with structured, downloadable, citable government fiscal data.

### Features

**1. Bulk Data Downloads**
- CSV/Excel exports of budget data, FAAC allocations, GovSpend payments, corruption cases
- Filterable by state, year, sector, MDA
- Pre-formatted for statistical analysis (Stata, R, Python-friendly)

**2. Saved Queries**
- Students save and revisit complex queries
- Share queries with supervisors/collaborators
- Version control on query parameters

**3. Citation-Ready Source References**
- Every data point includes:
  - Source document name and page
  - Data extraction date
  - OurNigeria dataset version
  - Suggested citation format (APA, Chicago, Harvard)
- Solves the academic requirement to cite primary sources

**4. Research Workspace**
- Personal dashboard with saved datasets, notes, and visualizations
- Collaborative features for research groups
- Export charts in publication-ready formats

**5. Methodology Documentation**
- How data was extracted, cleaned, and structured
- Known limitations and data gaps
- Recommended disclaimers for academic use

## Technical Requirements

1. **Data export engine** — CSV/Excel generation from database queries with proper formatting (1-2 weeks)
2. **User authentication** — .edu.ng email verification, faculty ID verification (1 week)
3. **Query workspace** — Saved queries, datasets, notes per user (2 weeks)
4. **Citation generator** — Auto-generate citation in multiple academic formats (3-5 days)
5. **Methodology docs** — Static pages documenting data provenance (3-5 days)

**Total estimated effort:** 5-6 weeks

---

## Campus Ambassador Program

Pair the Open Data Lab launch with a Campus Ambassador Program:
- Recruit 2-3 students per target university
- Train them on the platform and data literacy
- They organize workshops in their departments
- Ambassadors earn certificates and potential internship pipeline

This creates organic distribution within universities at minimal cost.
