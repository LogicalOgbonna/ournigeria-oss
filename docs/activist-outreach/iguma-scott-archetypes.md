## Step 2: Persona Filtering (Thinking)

*   **Thought Process:** The Nigerian Twitter space is highly polarized. I need to distinguish the target archetype (constructive, watchdog, positive change) from standard political trolls, official government spokespeople, or generic news aggregators.
*   **Action:** I established the following strict criteria for inclusion:
    *   **Bio Indicator:** Must have a bio indicating personal mission, advocacy, journalism, or civic tech (e.g., "Active Citizen," "Governance," "Accountability").
    *   **Content Originality:** Must post original commentary, videos, or on-the-ground reporting, not just retweets or copy-pasted news headlines.
    *   **Engagement Style:** Frequently tags or quotes official handles (e.g., @NigeriaGov, local LGAs, agencies) to demand answers or highlight progress, rather than just insulting them.
    *   **Follower Scale:** Micro to mid-tier (5k - 100k followers) preferred, though some macro-influencers who maintain a grassroots feel are acceptable.

## Step 3: Discovery & Verification (Thinking)

*   **Thought Process:** Using the criteria established, I analyzed potential candidates across the Nigerian civic tech and journalism space. I looked for individuals associated with organizations like BudgIT, Follow The Money, EiE Nigeria, and YIAGA Africa, as well as independent investigative journalists who focus on local issues.
