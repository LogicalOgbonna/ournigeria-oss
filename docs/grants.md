## Tier 1: Apply Immediately

### 1. Google.org Impact Challenge: AI for Government Innovation

**The single best opportunity for Aje right now.**

- **Amount:** $1M - $3M USD per project + Google Cloud credits (up to $200K) + multi-month accelerator with Google AI experts
- **Deadline:** April 3, 2026 (11:59 PM PT)
- **Apply at:** [google.org/impact-challenges/ai-government-innovation](https://www.google.org/impact-challenges/ai-government-innovation/)
- **Platform:** Applications submitted via [Submittable](https://www.google.org/impact-challenges/ai-government-innovation/)

**Why Aje fits perfectly:**
- AI-powered tool making government data accessible to citizens
- Directly helps government transparency and public service delivery
- Uses generative AI (Gemini) for civic good
- Demonstrates scalable impact across Nigeria's 36 states

**Eligibility:**
- Nonprofits, social enterprises, academic institutions, and for-profit businesses with clear social impact purpose
- Must demonstrate active collaboration with or government buy-in
- Must be registered in an eligible country

**Selection criteria:**
1. **Impact** — Address critical public service challenges with measurable outcomes
2. **Innovation** — Use generative or agentic AI aligned with Google's AI Principles
3. **Feasibility** — Realistic execution plan, capable team, documented government partnerships
4. **Scalability** — Potential for replication across governments and regions

**What they provide:**
- $1M-$3M in funding
- Up to $200K in Google Cloud Credits
- Multi-month Accelerator program
- Pro bono technical support from Google AI engineers

**How to apply:**
1. Visit the application page (link above)
2. Submit via Submittable platform
3. Applications reviewed by Google.org, Google subject matter experts, and external specialists from Apolitical and Centre for Public Impact

### 2. National Endowment for Democracy (NED)

**Fast turnaround, well-suited for early-stage civic tech.**

- **Amount:** Typically $30K-$150K (amount varies by project)
- **Next deadline:** June 2, 2026 (Board decision: September 11, 2026)
- **Apply at:** [ned.org/apply-for-grant/en](https://www.ned.org/apply-for-grant/en/)
- **Email for Africa proposals:** africaproposals@ned.org

**Why Aje fits:**
- NED specifically funds transparency and accountability initiatives in Nigeria
- They already fund Nigerian election reform and democratic governance partners
- Small but fast — good for early operational runway
- $50K covers ~2 years at 1,000 DAU on Gemini Flash

**Eligibility:**
- Must be a nongovernmental organization (civic group, association, independent media)
- Cannot fund individuals, governmental bodies, or public universities
- Organizations based in established democracies working on eligible countries are accepted

**Focus areas:**
- Human rights and rule of law
- Independent media and information freedom
- Democratic values and civic education
- Accountability, transparency, and conflict resolution
- Civil society strengthening

**Required documents:**
1. Organization Profile Form (PDF — download from NED website)
2. Application Proposal Form (PDF)
3. Proposal Narrative (max 15 pages, following NED guidelines)
4. Budget (Excel template provided on website)
5. Registration documents (or explanation if unregistered)

**How to apply:**
1. Download the required forms from [ned.org/apply-for-grant/en](https://www.ned.org/apply-for-grant/en/)
2. Write a proposal narrative (max 15 pages) covering:
   - Summary (1-2 paragraphs)
   - Background (1-2 pages)
   - Program description, objectives, activities, timeline
3. Complete the budget using their Excel template
4. Email all documents to **africaproposals@ned.org**
5. Or mail to: National Endowment for Democracy, Attn: Grant Proposals, 1201 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20004 USA

**Review cycle:**
- Decisions made 3x/year by NED Board of Directors
- Upcoming deadlines: June 2, 2026
- Late submissions roll to the next cycle

## Tier 2: Proactive Outreach Required

### 4. Luminate Group (Part of Omidyar Group)

**No open application — must get on their radar through networking.**

- **Amount:** $50K-$500K (unrestricted grants preferred)
- **Deadline:** No open call — Luminate identifies partners proactively
- **Website:** [luminategroup.com](https://www.luminategroup.com/)
- **Funding process:** [luminategroup.com/funding-process](https://www.luminategroup.com/funding-process)
- **Funding FAQ:** [luminategroup.com/funding-faq](https://www.luminategroup.com/funding-faq)

**Why Aje fits:**
- Luminate's four focus areas map directly to Aje: Civic Empowerment, Data & Digital Rights, Financial Transparency, and Independent Media
- They fund civic tech in the Global South specifically
- Active in Africa since 2009
- Recently granted to Cedar Seed Foundation (Nigeria, 2025) for civic participation

**Their four-stage process:**
1. **Introductory Discussions** — They reach out (or you get introduced), initial meeting
2. **Due Diligence** — Deeper assessment, stakeholder interviews, financial/legal review
3. **Funding Decision** — Internal approval, Funding Committee for large grants
4. **Approval & Agreement** — Legal review, funding agreement, partnership begins

**How to get noticed:**
- Build relationships with current Luminate grantees (BudgIT, CTIN, etc.)
- Present at civic tech conferences and forums they attend
- Publish impact reports and stories showing measurable outcomes
- Reach out via their website contact form with a strong introduction
- Connect through the Civic Tech Innovation Network (CTIN) which Luminate funds

**Timeline:** Goal is 3 months from first contact to funding decision (varies).

### 10. USAID (Caution: Funding Uncertainty)

**USAID funding to Nigeria has been significantly reduced.**

- **Current status:** Executive Order 14169 (Jan 2025) effectively ended 65 years of USAID assistance to Nigeria
- **Impact:** USAID funding to civic organisations dropped ~76% between 2023-2024, before the 2025 freeze
- **U.S. Embassy grants:** Small grants ($20K-$50K) may still be available through [ng.usembassy.gov/grants](https://ng.usembassy.gov/grants/)

**Recommendation:** Do not rely on USAID as a funding source given current political uncertainty. Monitor for changes.

## Pitch Template for All Applications

> **One-liner:** Aje is an AI-powered platform that makes Nigeria's $30B+ annual public spending data — previously locked in unreadable PDFs — searchable and understandable by any citizen, in English and Pidgin, via Telegram and web.
>
> **The problem:** Nigeria publishes FAAC allocations, state budgets, and government payment records, but the data is trapped in inconsistent PDF formats across hundreds of documents. No ordinary citizen can access, search, or understand this information.
>
> **The solution:** We built an AI pipeline that extracts, structures, and indexes government fiscal data — 84+ months of FAAC allocations, 891K+ government payment records, state budgets from 2019-2025, and EFCC corruption cases — making it conversationally queryable by any Nigerian in seconds.
>
> **The impact:** For the first time, a market woman in Aba can ask "how much did my LGA receive from FAAC last month?" and get a real answer in 5 seconds. Journalists, CSOs, and citizens can hold government accountable with data, not guesswork.
>
> **What we need:** Operational funding to cover AI inference costs and infrastructure ($350-$6,500/month depending on scale) so the platform remains 100% free for every Nigerian citizen.
