## Key Strategic Principles (from Timi)

1. **Make money from day one** — B2B revenue funds the B2C mission
2. **Lead with partner value** — show them how THEY make money, not how you charge them
3. **Start with quick wins** — online/tech publications first, traditional media later
4. **Two-entity structure** — for-profit research company (revenue) + non-profit foundation (civic mission), run in parallel
5. **Don't burn money on B2C alone** — content/marketing is essential for brand but must be funded by B2B revenue or grants
6. **The data alone isn't enough** — value comes from cleaning, structuring, enriching, and enabling workflows (not just making raw data easier to access)
7. **Validate before building** — talk to 10-20 potential customers before investing in enterprise features
