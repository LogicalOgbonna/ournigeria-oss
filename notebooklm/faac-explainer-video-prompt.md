# FAAC Explainer Video Prompt

Upload your FAAC PDF documents alongside this prompt.

---

Create a 1-minute video script for OurNigeria (ournigeria.ng). Explain FAAC allocation in clear, simple English — no jargon, no Pidgin. Like explaining to a friend who's never heard of FAAC. Use real numbers from the uploaded documents.

## Specs

Format: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920), TikTok/Reels/Shorts. Duration: 60s. Background: #080c0a. Accent: emerald #34d399. Numbers: IBM Plex Mono bold with emerald glow. Titles: Instrument Serif white. Body: DM Sans white 70% opacity. Captions: bottom with blur.

## Script Structure

### Scene 1: Hook (0:00-0:05)
Visual: Black screen, total counts up from N0 in emerald glow. Audio: "Every month, over one trillion naira is shared among Nigeria's states and local governments." Caption: "But how exactly is it divided?"

### Scene 2: What is FAAC? (0:05-0:15)
Visual: Animated container filling — "Oil Revenue", "VAT", "Customs", "Others" flowing in. Audio: "Oil revenue, VAT, customs duties — it all flows into one central Federation Account. FAAC is the committee that decides how to split it among Federal Government, 36 states + FCT, and 774 local governments." Caption: "FAAC = The body that divides Nigeria's revenue"

### Scene 3: The Split (0:15-0:25)
Visual: Donut chart animating segment by segment. Audio: "Federal Government takes 52.68%. States share 26.72%. Local governments get 20.60%. The centre always takes the largest portion." Caption: "Federal Government takes more than half"

### Scene 4: State Formula (0:25-0:35)
Visual: Five bars appearing one by one. Audio: "The states' share is divided by formula: 40% equally, 30% by population (why Lagos and Kano get more), 10% by land mass, 10% rewards states generating own revenue, 10% for social development needs." Caption: "Population + revenue effort = higher allocation"

### Scene 5: Oil Advantage (0:35-0:48)
Visual: Split screen — oil state (Rivers N45.2B, big emerald) vs non-oil (Ebonyi N8.1B, smaller). Audio: "The biggest factor? Oil. States producing oil get an extra 13% derivation on top. Rivers, Delta, Akwa Ibom receive two to three times more. Only 9 states qualify. The other 27 don't." Caption: "Oil states receive a bonus others cannot access"

### Scene 6: Deductions (0:48-0:53)
Visual: N20B shrinking to N15B, red "DEDUCTIONS" eating into it. Audio: "Even allocated funds aren't guaranteed. Debt repayments are deducted first. Some states lose 25% before seeing a naira." Caption: "Debt reduces what states actually receive"

### Scene 7: CTA (0:53-1:00)
Visual: app.ournigeria.ng + QR code + "36+FCT | 774 LGAs | 2019-2025". Audio: "Your state received money last month. Do you know how it was spent? Ask your own questions." Caption: "ournigeria.ng — Track Your Money"

## Extract from Documents

1. Most recent month's national total (hook number) 2. Top/bottom 3 states with exact amounts 3. Federal/State/LGA split 4. Oil state derivation totals 5. Biggest deductions 6. Month-over-month change. Replace ALL example figures with real data. Cite sources.

## Tone

Simple English, conversational. Every sentence has a number. Build energy: calm start → surprise at formula → peak at oil gap. Analogies: "FAAC is like splitting a bill — the formula decides shares" / "13% derivation is a finder's fee for oil states" / "Deductions are automatic loan repayments taken before money arrives."

## Rules

NO Pidgin. NO jargon. NO blue/purple colors. NO "approximately." NO hashtags in video. Currency: always naira sign (never NGN). Music: Afrobeat instrumental, 90-100 BPM, beat drop at 0:35, fade out at CTA.
